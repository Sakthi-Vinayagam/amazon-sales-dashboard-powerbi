# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance across different countries, product categories, brands, and payment methods. It provides key business insights through dynamic visualizations and interactive filters, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Identify top-selling products and categories.
- Compare sales across different countries.
- Analyze customer distribution.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Understand customer payment preferences.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Microsoft Excel

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest sales among all product categories.
- Credit Card was the most preferred payment method.
- The United States recorded the highest number of customer orders.
- Monthly sales remained relatively stable throughout the year with slight seasonal variations.
- Customer distribution across product categories was well balanced.
